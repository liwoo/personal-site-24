This is my personal website. I'm Jeremiah Chienda, based in the UK, and I'm the controller for the personal data the site collects. This page explains what that data is, why I have it, and who else touches it. The short version: I collect as little as I can, I don't sell it, and I don't run ads.

## What I collect

**When you send the contact form.** The fields you fill in, usually your name, email address and message, plus whatever the topic asks for (event details, budget, job type). It arrives in my inbox as an email. I use it to reply to you and for nothing else.

**When you join the newsletter.** Your email address, the name you give (if any) and the list you signed up to. I use it to send a welcome email and then the newsletter. Every newsletter issue has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing stops them straight away.

**When you browse.** Like every website, the host receives your IP address and browser details to serve the page, and keeps short-lived server logs for security. I also use Vercel Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and without identifying you. I see totals, such as how many people read a post, not who read it.

## Cookies and local storage

- **reCAPTCHA** (Google) runs on the contact and newsletter forms to stop spam. It may set cookies and reads browser and device signals to tell people from bots. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Your settings** are kept in your browser's local storage, not sent to me: your cookie choice, light or dark theme, and whether Dev Mode is on.
- **Embedded videos and posts.** A few articles embed YouTube videos or posts from X (Twitter). These load from YouTube and X as soon as you open the article, and those services may set their own cookies.

## Who else processes your data

- [Vercel](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy) hosts the site and provides the analytics.
- [Resend](https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy) delivers contact form emails and newsletter emails, and stores the newsletter list.
- [Google reCAPTCHA](https://policies.google.com/privacy) checks the forms for spam.
- [YouTube](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [X](https://x.com/en/privacy) serve the videos and posts embedded in some articles.

Some images, such as project covers, load from the project's own website or an image host, which sees your IP address like any web server.

These providers may process data outside the UK. When they do, they rely on safeguards recognised under UK data protection law, such as standard contractual clauses.

Booking a call takes you to [Cal.com](https://cal.com/privacy), and paid sessions are booked and paid for on a GeniYanga storefront. Those services handle your booking and payment details under their own privacy policies.

## Why I'm allowed to

- **Contact form:** to reply to a message you chose to send (legitimate interest, or steps towards a contract if you're asking to hire me).
- **Newsletter:** your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by unsubscribing.
- **Analytics, logs and spam protection:** legitimate interest in keeping the site working, secure and free of spam.

## How long I keep it

- Contact messages: as long as the conversation is useful, and no more than 2 years after we last spoke.
- Newsletter: until you unsubscribe.
- Analytics: aggregated counts only, kept by Vercel under its retention policy.

## Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data I hold about you, object to how I use it, or ask me to restrict it. [Send me a message](/contact) and I'll reply within a month. If you're unhappy with how I've handled your data, you can complain to the [Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).

## Changes

If I change what the site collects, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
