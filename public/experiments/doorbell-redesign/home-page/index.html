Five changes that matter

1

Lead with the delivery task

Use a plain “Deliver to” address row. Give the search field a stable, explicit label covering all shopping services.

2

Reduce competing content

Show useful shops before advertising. Keep one compact offer entry; move merchant recruitment and social feeds to Account.

3

Make the choice readable

Replace the nine-logo block with shop cards showing rating, delivery time and fee. Use 14–16sp metadata and 12–13sp menu labels.

4

Keep navigation in place

Five equally weighted, labelled destinations. Keep the bottom bar visible while browsing; reserve space so it never covers content.

5

Use one visual language

Keep retro buttons, illustrated services and a compact ticket-style offer. Use quiet fields and dividers so those signature moments stand out.

Implementation order & evidence

Start with clarity. Then improve discovery.

This is a reviewable design recommendation, not a production change. Detailed source findings and acceptance checks are in audit.md.

P0 · Navigation and legibility

  • Keep bottom navigation visible and above reserved content space. Preserve existing multi-cart behavior and guest sign-in gates.
  • Increase 9sp bottom labels and 10sp service labels. Keep service names visible whenever their icons remain on screen.
  • Use explicit “Deliver to” context and a fixed search prompt. Keep search pinned; allow the service row to scroll away as a labelled unit.
  • Measure text contrast against actual backgrounds; target 4.5:1 for normal text. Use 48dp touch targets and verify 200% text scaling.

P1 · Reduce clutter

  • Show shops before promotional material. Replace the nine-logo Top Rated block with a small number of comparable shop cards and See all.
  • Move merchant recruitment and social feeds into Account. Use one compact offers entry; put eligibility details on the offer page.
  • Keep active-order tracking and genuinely critical service notices visible when applicable. Replace the offline takeover of navigation with an inline reconnect state.
  • Distinguish a loaded, empty section from a section that is still loading; empty promotions must not shimmer forever.

P1–P2 · Search and menus

  • Restore recent queries with Clear. Remove moving trends and random category reshuffling. Show loading, no-match and connection-error states separately.
  • Keep shops and products; add a combined results view. Support full shop result counts/pagination before displaying “See all”.
  • Add reliable filters, server-backed availability and location-aware fees. Preparation time alone must not be presented as a full delivery ETA.
  • Add search within a restaurant and a full category index; keep query, filters and scroll position when returning from a menu.

Competitor reference patterns

  • Uber Eats: delivery-address entry and browsing by cuisine or dish.
  • Deliveroo: location-first entry across restaurants, supermarkets and shops.
  • Wolt: cross-catalogue search, grouped results, search history, filtering and in-venue search.
  • W3C: contrast and resizing reference. These do not establish that the current app passes or fails a full accessibility audit.
